— Who We Are

Born in the Streets

Limelight LL started with a simple belief: everyone deserves their moment in the spotlight. We make clothing for those who move through the city with intent — bold enough to be seen, sharp enough to be remembered.

Every piece is designed and produced locally. From the Old English lettering on the front panel to the smiley-face graphics that carry our signature energy — this is Uganda's streetwear, built for the world.
